# Fraud Pattern  
Scam conversations targeting the elderly, often involving synthetic data and machine learning models, exhibit distinct linguistic and behavioral patterns. These scams span eight categories (e.g., financial fraud, identity theft) and are simulated using a multi-agent LLM framework, though they may lack emotional nuances present in real-world interactions.  

# Executive Summary  
This study introduces the **COVA dataset**, a synthetic collection of 3,201 scam conversations targeting the elderly, and evaluates detection models for identifying scam outcomes. Key findings include the superiority of **XGBoost + TF-IDF** (72.5% accuracy) over transformers like DistilBERT, which face overfitting and input length constraints. Limitations include synthetic data’s lack of real-world emotional dynamics and US-centric, English-only scope. Future work aims to expand the dataset, improve real-time detection, and enhance multilingual generalization.  

# Definition  
Scam conversations targeting the elderly are fraudulent interactions designed to exploit cognitive vulnerabilities, often involving deceptive linguistic patterns, urgency, and emotional manipulation. The COVA dataset simulates these interactions across eight categories (e.g., financial fraud, identity theft) using a multi-agent LLM framework.  

# Typical Attack Workflow  
1. **Scam Simulation**: Generate synthetic conversations using a multi-agent LLM framework.  
2. **Behavioral Modeling**: Replicate scammer tactics (e.g., urgency, false authority) and victim responses (e.g., hesitation, compliance).  
3. **Outcome Labeling**: Classify interactions into three categories: "victim falls for scam," "rejects scam," or "engages without committing."  
4. **Model Evaluation**: Test detection models on labeled data, with TF-IDF-based approaches outperforming transformers in large-scale scenarios.  

# Behavioural Characteristics  
- **Scammer Tactics**: Use of urgency, false authority, and emotional manipulation.  
- **Victim Responses**: Hesitation, compliance, or rejection, often influenced by linguistic cues (e.g., persuasive language).  
- **Synthetic Limitations**: Lack of real-world emotional nuance and global/multilingual representation.  

# Indicators  
- **Linguistic Patterns**: Persuasive language, urgent requests, and fabricated authority claims.  
- **Conversation Dynamics**: Sudden shifts in tone, repeated questioning, or pressure to act immediately.  
- **Model Performance**: XGBoost + TF-IDF achieves 72.5% accuracy, while transformers show overfitting and input length constraints.  

# Common Feature Patterns  
- **TF-IDF Superiority**: Outperforms transformers in large-scale conversational tasks due to input length and training data size limitations.  
- **3-Class Collapse**: Merging behavioral distinctions into three classes may obscure critical nuances for intervention systems.  
- **Synthetic Data Gaps**: Misses emotional dynamics and global/multilingual scam interactions.  

# Detection Signals  
- **Model-Based Signals**: TF-IDF features (e.g., keyword frequency, n-grams) and transformer embeddings (though limited by input length).  
- **Behavioral Signals**: Urgency in language, abrupt tonal shifts, and pressure to act.  
- **Outcome Prediction**: Classification into "fall for scam," "reject," or "engage without committing."  

# False Positives  
- **Transformer Overfitting**: Fine-tuned models may misclassify due to insufficient training data (3,201 conversations).  
- **Labeling Inconsistencies**: 9.9% of conversations (317) remain uncorrected due to low confidence in validation.  
- **Synthetic Data Bias**: Lack of real-world emotional cues may lead to false negatives in real-world deployment.  

# Prevention  
- **Dataset Expansion**: Grow COVA to over 10,000 conversations to improve transformer scalability.  
- **Real-Time Detection**: Develop incremental classification models for partial transcript prediction.  
- **Multilingual Support**: Add non-English conversations and new scam categories for global generalization.  
- **Public Release**: Share COVA dataset, generation pipeline, and detection baselines for broader validation.
